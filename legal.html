Privacy Notice

Data controller. The controller of personal data collected through this site is KS Capital Partners S.r.l., reachable at d.kapo@kscapitalpartners.it.

Data collected. This site hosts no forms and collects no data through its pages: there are no fields to complete, no sign-ups and no restricted areas. The only channel of contact is the published email address. In writing to it you send us your own email address, any signature block, and the content of your message. IP address and browser type may be recorded in the logs of the server hosting the site, for security purposes and for the technically necessary period. No special categories of data are requested; please do not send any.

Purpose and legal basis. Data is processed to answer the enquiry, to assess whether an introduction is possible, and to hold the correspondence that follows. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, steps taken at your request before entering into a contract, and Article 6(1)(f) GDPR, the legitimate interest in responding to business correspondence and keeping a record of it.

Retention. Enquiry data is kept for the time needed to reply and, where a relationship follows, for the duration of that relationship and any period required by law. Enquiries that lead to no further contact are deleted within twenty-four months.

Disclosure. Data is not sold, rented or passed to third parties for marketing. It is available only to persons acting for the controller and to the providers hosting the site and the email account, appointed as processors and bound by confidentiality.

Your rights. Under Articles 15 to 22 GDPR you may request access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing or portability, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interest. To exercise these rights, write to d.kapo@kscapitalpartners.it; a reply follows within one month. You may also lodge a complaint with the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali.

Cookie Notice

This site uses technical cookies only. They are necessary for the pages to load and for the site to stay secure. It stores no preference in your browser: the two language versions are separate pages at separate addresses, and choosing a language records nothing. The technical cookies do not identify you. Neither is used to identify you.

No profiling cookies are set. No advertising, remarketing or social media cookies are set, and no third-party tracking scripts are embedded in the pages. Because only technical cookies and a language preference are involved, no consent banner is required under Article 122 of the Italian Privacy Code and the Garante's cookie guidelines.

If measurement tools are added later, this notice will be updated first. Analytics that are not fully anonymised, and any cookie set by a third party, would require prior consent through a banner offering a genuine refusal, and would run only after that consent. Stored preferences can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.
