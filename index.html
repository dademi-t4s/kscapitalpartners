Who we are

The introduction is the work.

KS Capital Partners holds relationships built over years and places them where they are needed. We do not invest, do not intermediate financial instruments and do not manage capital.

We are independent. No mandate on either side, no ownership, no position in the outcome. That neutrality is why a door opens: there is no side for us to favour, and both parties know what we are and what we are not before the first call. We are paid for the introduction and the relationship behind it.

A well-made introduction is not a contact passed along. Both sides are qualified before either is named: mandate, capacity, funds, provenance, title. Each learns of the other only when both have agreed to be known. Nothing is published: no names, no figures, no case studies. Discretion is not a clause here. It is the product.

We move at the speed of trust. Slower than the market, and more durable.

How we work

Four steps, taken in order

An introduction is prepared long before it is made. Each stage is deliberate, and any of them may end the matter. We would rather decline early than spend a principal's afternoon.

  1. 01

    Reading the mandate

    We listen first, then test what we heard. Scope, capacity, timing, title. If the requirement is not real, or not yet real, we say so and the matter closes there.

  2. 02

    Mapping the room

    We know who sits where, and who will actually take the call. The right name is chosen, approached quietly, and confirmed as available before either party is told anything.

  3. 03

    The introduction

    Names are exchanged only once both sides have agreed to be known. Terms of confidentiality are set before the first call. What is said in the room stays with the principals.

  4. 04

    Alongside

    We stay reachable while the relationship finds its own shape. Where a question of language, culture or timing arises, we answer it. The transaction belongs to the principals.

We give no investment, legal or tax advice, and we hold no position in what follows.

The network

People, not a database

The network is a small number of relationships held over years, across Europe, the Mediterranean basin, the Gulf and North America. Each one is maintained in person, in the counterparty's own language and business culture. We make an introduction only where we are known well enough for the call to be taken.

Names are not published, here or anywhere. The network is described in general terms and disclosed only to the party it concerns, with consent.
